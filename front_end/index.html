How CropEazy works

From login to harvest planning — here is the full journey of how predictions, alerts, and your personal dashboard come together.

Step 01

Sign in with OTP

Enter your name and mobile number. We send a one-time password to verify you, so each farmer gets a private account and separate dashboard records.

Step 02

Allow GPS location

CropEazy reads your coordinates to auto-fill temperature, humidity, rainfall, and your region — so you spend less time typing climate data.

Step 03

Choose your prediction mode

Recommend a crop uses soil nutrients and climate to suggest the best crop. Predict production estimates total output in tonnes from farm area, crop type, and regional parameters — all powered by trained ML models.

Step 04

Review pests & advisories

After prediction, you see a seasonal pest calendar showing which insects and diseases are most likely during each month and Kharif / Rabi / Zaid season for your crop.

Step 05

Enable emergency SMS alerts

If a flood, heatwave, or storm is forecast near your field, CropEazy sends an SMS to your verified mobile number so you can protect crops in time.

Step 06

Track profit & loss in ₹

Production predictions are saved to your dashboard with revenue, costs, and net profit in Indian Rupees — including seed, labour, fertiliser, and market price inputs.
